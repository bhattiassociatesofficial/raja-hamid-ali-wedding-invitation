### Phase 5 — what was found, fixed and tested

**Cause (exact).** Barat is the front card of the pocket: `slotB = D − 1 − Ti/2`, so its stock face sits 1 px behind Main's front face. Every card's printed panel (`.mount`) is raised `0.0045 · h` off its card (2.91 px at 390×844), so Barat's panel ended **+1.91 px in front of Main's face** (1.00 px behind Main's own panel). While Barat slid in or out of the pocket, its panel was correctly depth-sorted in front of Main's top frame band. Walima is seated `Ti + 1.2` px deeper, so its panel is 3.17 px behind Main's face and never showed. No sorting ambiguity: the planes are parallel (rx = 0) and the order is constant from p = 0.92436.
Where: `goBack(T.barat, M.bBack)` (`bBack = [0.89147, 0.93846]`), move 4 `outP → pocket` (p 0.92342–0.93846, `sine`), z = `slot` throughout; first overlay pixel p = 0.92670, last p = 0.93820 (Barat is hidden from 0.93825). The earlier "clean at 0.9365" was only the ornament leaving: the blank white panel strip stayed over Main's band until 0.9382. The same constant caused the mirror overlay on extraction (p 0.2245–0.2405).
**Fix.** One expression in `buildTracks()` (`js/app.js`): `insertTrack(slotB - 0.0045 * Hi, …)`. Walima, Family, camera, timing, sizes and typography are untouched; the 5a z-clearance is kept.
Full report: `docs/barat-pocket-depth-2026-10-08/DIAGNOSIS.md` (local evidence; not in git).

### Phase 7 — tap-to-advance experience (2026-10-10)

**Why.** Guests did not discover that the invitation had to be scrolled. The story is now moved by taps; scrolling does nothing.
**Starting point.** The working tree held an uncommitted, untested tap-driven controller from an earlier session (snapshot `backups/2026-10-10-pre-tap-wip/`, build `79b74e5e…`, 651,520 B). Phase 7 reviewed it against the brief, fixed what testing found, tested it end to end and shipped it. The choreography itself (camera, cards, pocket depth, zoom geometry, typography, artwork, content) is the Phase 6 engine unchanged: the scene is still a pure function of `p`.

**Pages (rest states).** 0 closed Invitation · 1 Main invitation · 2 Barat · 3 Walima · 4 Guests ("Looking Forward", the 12 names in their configured order) · 5 Thank You (closed again). No Groom card; nothing combined, omitted or reordered.
**Interaction.** A tap anywhere on the scene (or on the hint text, or Enter/Space/→/PageDown) advances exactly one page; the controller accepts a tap only at rest, so taps during a movement are ignored (never queued). Nothing advances by itself: the finished page stays until the next tap. Back (bottom left; ←/PageUp) plays the same page in reverse. The page cannot scroll, so wheel/swipe/trackpad never skip anything. The close-up (magnifier button or Z; drag/wheel to look; ✕ / Esc / a tap to leave) keeps working and never advances; a tap on the card no longer enters it because a tap now means "next page". Rotation/resizing only re-measure, the page and the movement carry on.
**Hint.** "Tap to open" on the closed card; "Tap to continue" on pages 1–3, "Tap to finish" on page 4; none on the Thank You page (it has "Open again"). Shown only while a tap would be accepted (the page is complete, 0.7 s after the last movement), fades in over 1.3 s, breathes slowly, and is gone within 0.2 s of a tap, in the close-up and in reduced motion.
**Pace (plan, seconds; measured on a GPU Chrome in parentheses).** Tap 1 closed → Main 18.3 (19.95 incl. a one-off start-up stall); tap 2 → Barat 13.3 (13.30); tap 3 → Walima 13.3 (13.61); tap 4 → Guests 35.7 (36.73); tap 5 → Thank You 8.4 (8.46); about 89 s in all. The transition always finishes before the writing starts (checked per page). Writing: names 6–10 glyphs/s (each guest name 2.2 s: written large, held, set in place), headings 8–16, body lines 25–28 (were 25–43), digits 15. Playback-rate changes happen where the scene is still: the largest speed step at any of the 12 joins is 0.6 px/s at 390×844 and 1280×800. Reduced motion: each page whole, 0.3 s fade out / 0.5 s fade in per tap.

**Found and fixed in Phase 7.** (1) *Desktop lid snap:* with the mouse over the card (always the case when clicking) the hover lift of 9° dropped to 0° on the first tap, then the lid opened: now the tap takes the lift over and the lid only ever opens further (old build: a rise of exactly 9.000° in one step; new: monotonic). (2) *Accidental restart:* "Open again" appears where "Tap to finish" was, so an impatient guest's extra taps during the closing could restart the invitation (reproduced on the previous build): it now answers only when the farewell has rested 1.5 s and never while the ending moves. (3) The hint's fade-out is 0.2 s (was 0.4 s) so it is gone while a tap would be ignored. (4) Body-text writing slowed to 25–28 glyphs/s. (5) The hint and Back are 12.5/11.5 px (were 11/10.5) on large pointer screens.
